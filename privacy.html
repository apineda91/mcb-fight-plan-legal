MCB Fighter Plan Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

MCB Fighter Plan is a training, weight and nutrition log for boxers. This policy explains what the app collects, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions: ajpineda@umich.edu.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressTo create your account and send sign-in codes
Training sessions (activity, rounds, minutes, effort, distance, notes)To show your training log and workload
Weigh-insTo track weight against your goal
Meals (calories, protein, carbs, fat, water)To track nutrition against your targets
Camp plan (fight date, goal weight, daily targets)To calculate countdowns and pace

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers, and the app has no ads or analytics. Reminder times stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • Your team's coach. Accounts marked as coach can view (but not change or delete) athletes' entries, so they can plan training and monitor weight cuts.
  • Service providers that run the app: Supabase stores the data and sends sign-in emails; Expo delivers app updates (it doesn't receive your logs).

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else, except if required by law.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Database rules restrict each account to its own data, plus read-only access for coaches.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. You can:

  • delete single entries in the app (trash icon next to an entry);
  • erase all your entries: Plan → Danger zone → Delete everything;
  • delete your account and all its data: Plan → Danger zone → Delete my account. This is immediate and permanent.

No access to the app? See how to request deletion.

Health information

The app is a training log, not medical advice or a medical device. Talk to a doctor or your team's medical staff about weight cutting and nutrition.

Age

MCB Fighter Plan is for users 18 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
