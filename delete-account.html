Delete your MCB Fight Plan account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open MCB Fight Plan and go to the Plan tab.
  2. Under Danger zone, turn on I want to delete all my data.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email ajpineda@umich.edu from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my MCB Fight Plan account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your email address, every training session, weigh-in and meal, and your camp plan. Nothing is kept afterwards. Reminder settings live only on your phone; uninstalling the app removes them.
